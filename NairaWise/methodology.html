Data sources and methodology

This page explains what data NairaWise uses, how it checks it, and the limits you should know about.

Sources in order of use

OrderSourceWhat it is
1Frankfurter API, CBN providerThe Central Bank of Nigeria USD/NGN “official exchange rate” series, relayed by Frankfurter. Your browser asks for it directly.
2NairaWise data fileA saved copy of the same series, refreshed twice a day by an automated job and checked before saving. Used when the browser cannot reach the source.
3Your browser’s saved copyThe last valid rate this browser downloaded. Used when 1 and 2 are not available.

If none is available, the page says so and you can retry or use your own rate.

How dates and freshness work

  • Rate date is the date reported by the source. It is never replaced by the date on your device.
  • Last checked is when this browser or the automated job really downloaded the data, shown in West Africa Time (WAT). If the time is not known, the page says so.
  • Stale warning appears when the rate date is more than 3 calendar days old.
  • Saved copies are labelled as saved. They are never shown as freshly retrieved.

Checks before a rate is accepted

  • The response must be valid JSON for the pair USD to NGN.
  • The rate must be a finite number above zero, with a valid calendar date that is not in the future.
  • Requests time out after 8 seconds and duplicate requests are blocked.
  • A failed or invalid response never overwrites a good saved rate.

What NairaWise does not claim

  • It is not a live or real-time rate. The source series is a daily one.
  • It is not confirmed to be the NFEM rate.
  • It is not the rate a bank, bureau de change or other dealer will give you.
  • There is no automated parallel-market rate. We found no free source that clearly allows public display, and we do not scrape websites.

Attribution

Exchange-rate data: Central Bank of Nigeria, accessed through Frankfurter. NairaWise is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.

Privacy

NairaWise has no accounts, ads or tracking scripts. Amounts you type stay in your browser. Your browser stores one small item (the last valid rate, its date, its source and the time it was downloaded) so the page can work offline.
