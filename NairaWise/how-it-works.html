How the USD to NGN converter works

The NairaWise calculator turns dollars into naira (and back) with one multiplication. This page explains each step so you know exactly what the number on screen means.

The formula

Naira amount = US dollar amount × naira per US dollar.

To go the other way, NairaWise divides: dollars = naira amount ÷ naira per US dollar.

A worked example

To show the maths we will use a made-up round rate of ₦1,000 per $1. It is not a real exchange rate.

You typeCalculationResult
$5050 × 1,000₦50,000
$250.50250.50 × 1,000₦250,500
₦75,000 (swapped)75,000 ÷ 1,000$75

Which rate is used?

You choose. By default the converter uses the CBN-sourced reference rate, with its rate date shown beside the result. If you choose My own rate, the converter uses the number you type and labels it clearly as yours. It is never presented as a market rate or a CBN rate.

Rounding

NairaWise keeps the full rate in the calculation and only rounds what it displays: two decimal places for money, and up to four for the rate. Tiny amounts show more decimals so they do not appear as zero.

Input rules

  • Numbers only. You may use commas (1,250.50).
  • The amount must be more than zero and not larger than 1 trillion.
  • A dealer’s rate must be more than zero and not larger than 100,000.
  • Problems are explained in a message under the box.

Buttons and keyboard

  • ⇄ switches between dollars→naira and naira→dollars and carries your current answer across.
  • Quick amounts fill the box with common values.
  • Reset clears everything and returns to the reference rate.
  • Everything works with the Tab, Space and Enter keys.

What the result is not

The result is an estimate from a rate, not an offer. A bank, bureau de change or online platform may use a different rate and add fees. Always confirm the final amount with them first.
