Contact and feedback

We welcome corrections and ideas, including better Hausa wording.

How to send feedback

Open an issue on the project’s GitHub page: github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/YOUR-REPO/issues. A free GitHub account is needed.

What to include

  • The page address and what you expected to see.
  • The rate, rate date and source shown on the page, if your report is about a rate.
  • Your device and browser, if something does not work.

Please do not post private information such as bank details, passwords or phone numbers.
