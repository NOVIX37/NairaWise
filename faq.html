Frequently asked questions

What does NairaWise do?

NairaWise converts US dollars (USD) to Nigerian naira (NGN), and naira to dollars, using a reference exchange rate with its real date. You can also type in the rate your own dealer quotes.

Where does the exchange rate come from?

The automatic rate is the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) series passed on by the free Frankfurter service. The page always shows the rate date and where the number came from. See Data sources and methodology for details.

Is this the rate I will get from a bank or bureau de change?

Not necessarily. A reference rate is a benchmark. Banks, bureaux de change and online platforms set their own rates and fees, so the amount you receive can be different. Always ask for a quote before you trade.

What is the difference between the official/reference rate and the parallel-market rate?

The official/reference rate is published by the central bank. The parallel-market (or street) rate is what private dealers quote outside official channels, and it can differ by dealer, place and time. Read Understanding official and parallel-market rates for a fuller explanation.

Why does NairaWise not show a parallel-market rate?

We looked for a free source that clearly allows public display and found none. We will not scrape websites or guess a number. Instead, choose “My own rate” and type the rate your dealer gives you.

How often is the rate updated?

The source series usually changes once per business day. NairaWise checks it when you open the page or press “Refresh rate”, and a scheduled job saves a copy twice a day. The rate is not live or real-time.

Why is the rate date earlier than today?

The source may not publish on weekends and public holidays, and a new rate may appear later in the day. NairaWise shows the date the source reports, never the date on your device.

What do “Saved site data file” and “Saved in this browser” mean?

They mean NairaWise could not download a fresh rate just now, so it is showing an earlier saved one. The rate date and a warning (if it is more than 3 days old) tell you how old it is.

Does NairaWise cost money or collect my data?

It is free. There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Your browser keeps one small saved copy of the last rate so the page still works offline. Amounts you type are not sent anywhere.

Can I convert naira to dollars?

Yes. Press the ⇄ button beside the amount box. NairaWise divides your naira amount by the rate.
