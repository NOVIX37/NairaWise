How to convert dollars to naira

  1. Type the amount in dollars, or tap a quick amount such as $100.
  2. Read the result in naira. The rate and its date are shown under the result.
  3. Want naira to dollars? Press ⇄. Have a dealer quote? Choose “My own rate” and type it in.

The formula is simple: naira = dollars × naira per $1. See how the converter works for worked examples.

Dollar to naira today: what the rate on this page means

People search for “dollar to naira today” and expect one number. In reality there are several rates, and they answer different questions.

  • CBN-sourced reference rate. The Central Bank of Nigeria’s published USD/NGN rate, passed on by Frankfurter. It is dated and usually changes once per business day. It is shown automatically.
  • Dealer or street rate. What a bank, bureau de change or other dealer will actually offer you. It can be higher or lower than the reference rate, and fees may apply. Type it into “My own rate”.

NairaWise does not call its reference rate “live”. It is a daily figure, and the page tells you its date and when it was last checked (in West Africa Time). Read understanding official and parallel-market rates to see why the numbers differ.

Quick answers

Is this the rate I will get from a bank or bureau de change?

Not necessarily. A reference rate is a benchmark. Banks, bureaux de change and online platforms set their own rates and fees, so the amount you receive can be different. Always ask for a quote before you trade.

What is the difference between the official/reference rate and the parallel-market rate?

The official/reference rate is published by the central bank. The parallel-market (or street) rate is what private dealers quote outside official channels, and it can differ by dealer, place and time. Read Understanding official and parallel-market rates for a fuller explanation.

Why does NairaWise not show a parallel-market rate?

We looked for a free source that clearly allows public display and found none. We will not scrape websites or guess a number. Instead, choose “My own rate” and type the rate your dealer gives you.
